## Full description

```
Looty is a text-first social app for college students in India.

It is a friends app. It is not a dating app. There is no swipe, no crush,
and no “someone likes you”.

How it works
• Sign in with your email. That opens groups, chats and Looty Match.
• Confirm a college email if you have one. That adds a College Verified badge.
• Talk in global rooms: Study, Sports, Friends. Text only.
• Add people by username. Direct messages unlock when both of you accept.
• Looty Match shows you other students. Loot or pass. If you both loot, you
  are Connected and can chat.

Safety
• Bans are automatic. Reporting and blocking are separate.
• You can delete your account from You → Delete account.
• You choose which notifications to get.

Who it is for
College students in India. A college mailbox is optional — it shows as
College Verified. If your college is missing from the list, request it in
the app.

Android only.
```
