## Full description

```
Looty is a text-first social app for verified college students in India.

It is a friends app. It is not a dating app. There is no swipe, no crush,
and no “someone likes you”.

How it works
• Sign in and confirm a college email. That is what proves you are a student.
• Talk in global rooms: Study, Sports, Friends. Text only.
• Add people by username. Direct messages unlock when both of you accept.
• Looty Match shows you other verified students. Loot or pass. If you both
  loot, you are Connected and can chat.

Safety
• Bans are automatic. Reporting and blocking are separate.
• You can delete your account from You → Delete account.
• You choose which notifications to get.

Who it is for
Students whose college issues an email address we recognise. If yours is
missing, request it in the app — you can still read the rooms.

Android only.
```
