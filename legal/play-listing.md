## Full description

```
Looty is a text-first social app for college students in India.

It is a friends app. It is not a dating app. There is no swipe, no crush,
and no “someone likes you”.

How it works
• Sign in with your email. That opens groups, chats and Looty Match.
• Confirm a college email if you have one. That adds a College Verified badge.
• Make a private group, name it, and share its invite code. Text only.
• Add people by username. Direct messages unlock when both of you accept.
• Looty Match shows you other students. Loot or pass. If you both loot, you
  are Connected and can chat.

Safety
• Bans are automatic. Reporting and blocking are separate.
• You can delete your account from You → Delete account.
• You choose which notifications to get.

Who it is for
College students in India, aged 18 and over. A college mailbox is optional —
it shows as College Verified. If your college is missing from the list,
request it in the app.

Android only.
```

## Content rating notes (for the questionnaire)

Re-read 2026-09-06. Two things changed since the last pass: the app is 18+, and
groups are private and user-created rather than global public rooms.

Declare the target audience as **18 and over**. Note honestly that the age check
is a self-declared date of birth and is not verified — do not describe it as
age verification.

- Users interact with each other (chat, groups, discovery)
- Users can share photos in 1:1 chats (not in groups)
- Users can report and block
- Open signup: any confirmed email, not college-gated
- **18+ only, by self-declared date of birth at sign-up**
- **Groups are private and invite-only.** There is no directory and no way to
  find a group you were not given a code for. Group content is not public.
- No dating / romantic matching
- No location sharing
- **Ads are personalised** (changed 2026-09-06, and only defensible because the
  audience is declared 18+; if the age gate ever goes, these must go back to
  non-personalised)
- No user-to-user payments inside the social features (Play Billing later for
  optional extras: more loots / ad-free)
